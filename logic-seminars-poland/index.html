Coverage and sources

This directory records the public seminar information found in the institutions below. It is not a guarantee that every local or privately announced reading group has been found. “Published slot” means that an official page gives a meeting time; it does not confirm a meeting every week.

Institutions checked and gaps in the public schedules

Warsaw. University of Warsaw mathematics, computer science and philosophy; IMPAN; Warsaw University of Technology; UKSW; IPI PAN. The verified series are listed above. No separate current public logic seminar timetable was located for the other institutions checked.

Wrocław. University of Wrocław mathematics, computer science and philosophy; Wrocław University of Science and Technology. The joint set theory seminar is current. The model theory timetable needs confirmation. An additional formal-proving course was indexed, but its direct page could not be verified.

Kraków. Jagiellonian University mathematics, computer science and philosophy; AGH; University of the National Education Commission. High Noon and Foundations of Computer Science are listed above. No further current public logic research timetable was located. Degree-course seminars are not treated as open research meetings.

Łódź. University of Łódź mathematics, computer science and philosophy; Łódź University of Technology. ExtenDD and the Real Analysis Seminar have current programmes. Density Topologies has no public slot on the departmental page.

Gdańsk. University of Gdańsk mathematics, computer science and philosophy; Gdańsk University of Technology. The Weekly Math Seminar is current. Gdańsk Logic Colloquium Talks has a 2023 archive, not a confirmed current timetable.

Other centres. Poznań UAM; Toruń UMK; Kielce UJK; Katowice UŚ; Lublin KUL and UMCS; Opole; Rzeszów; Białystok. Verified series and uncertain schedules are listed above. No current dedicated public logic research timetable was located for the remaining centres in this search.
